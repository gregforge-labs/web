<p>This page describes cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies used by this Gregforge Labs LLC website (the static pages hosted at <strong>astrodyuti.com</strong>). It does <strong>not</strong> describe in-app tracking inside the AstroDyuti mobile application; for that, see <a href="/privacy.html">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

<h2 id="1-website-cookies">1. Website cookies</h2>
<p>This site is a set of static HTML pages hosted on a third-party static-hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages). <strong>We do not place first-party analytics, advertising, or personalization cookies on this site.</strong> We do not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, or any other behavioral-tracking script.</p>

<h2 id="2-operational-cookies-our-host-may-set">2. Operational cookies our host may set</h2>
<p>The static-hosting provider may set strictly necessary cookies, such as a load-balancer session cookie or a security/anti-abuse cookie, to deliver pages to your browser. These cookies are managed by the host (currently GitHub, Inc.) under its own privacy policy. We do not read or share them.</p>

<h2 id="3-embedded-content">3. Embedded content</h2>
<p>The website serves its Newsreader and Inter fonts from the same host as these pages. Loading a page does not make a separate Google Fonts request. We do not embed YouTube, Vimeo, social-media widgets, or third-party comment systems on these pages.</p>

<h2 id="4-do-not-track-global-privacy-control">4. Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control</h2>
<p>Because we do not run analytics, advertising, or personalization on this site, there is nothing here to honor or ignore a DNT or GPC signal against. For in-app data, where applicable, we honor the Universal Opt-Out Mechanism signals (including Global Privacy Control) as required by California, Colorado, and Connecticut law.</p>

<h2 id="5-changes">5. Changes</h2>
<p>If we ever add analytics or a content-management tool that uses cookies, we will update this page first and add a banner before any non-essential cookie is set.</p>
