<p>Interpretive content in AstroDyuti is informed by the classical Indian Jyotish literature, including (without limitation) Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra, Phaladīpikā, Saravali, Jātaka Pārijāta, Bṛhat Jātaka, Jaimini Sūtras, Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi, Muhūrta Mārtāṇḍa, and Vāstu Śāstra works.
